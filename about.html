About ApolloVX

ApolloVX was founded with a mission to modernize vending through technology, reliability, and exceptional service. We focus on delivering a seamless experience for both businesses and healthcare facilities.

Our approach is simple: provide smart, data-driven vending solutions that enhance convenience, reduce operational burden, and promote healthy choices.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative vending solutions that prioritize customer convenience, safety, and operational efficiency.

Our Vision

To become the trusted partner for businesses and healthcare facilities seeking modern, reliable, and technology-enabled vending solutions.

Our Values

Integrity, reliability, innovation, and customer-first service guide every decision we make and every machine we deploy.

Apollo Vending Experience

Modern vending solutions delivering convenience, reliability, and smart service for workplaces, schools, and public spaces.
