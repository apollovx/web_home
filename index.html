Our machines

Two ways to vend. One team running both.

We recommend the right mix for each location: classic machines where they fit, AI coolers where people need fresh food and a faster checkout.

Traditional vending

Classic machines, modernized.

Familiar snack and beverage machines fitted with cashless readers and live inventory telemetry, so they're stocked and working whenever someone walks up.

  • Apple Pay, Google Pay and credit cards
  • Real-time stock and uptime monitoring
  • Curated snacks, drinks and healthy options
Best for: break rooms, waiting areas, lobbies and corridors
AI smart vending

Tap, open, grab, go.

Customers tap a card or phone to unlock the door, take what they want, and close it. The machine recognizes each item and charges automatically. No buttons, no waiting.

  • Fresh meals, salads and larger items
  • Checkout in seconds, even at peak times
  • Sales data that shapes every restock
Best for: airport gates, staff lounges and 24/7 areas
