Apollo VX SMS Opt-In Process

Apollo VX provides vending machine services and support. Customers may contact our automated support phone line to report vending machine issues.

How Customers Opt In

Customers opt in to receive SMS notifications by calling the Apollo VX support phone line and reporting a vending machine issue.

  • Customer calls the Apollo VX support phone number
  • The system informs the caller that they may receive a transactional SMS message related to their request
  • The customer voluntarily provides their phone number during the support process
  • A confirmation SMS message is sent after the request is submitted

SMS Notification Details

SMS messages sent by Apollo VX are strictly transactional and relate to vending machine service requests.

  • Message frequency varies depending on service activity
  • Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier
  • Messages are not used for marketing or promotional purposes
